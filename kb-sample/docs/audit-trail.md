# Audit trail

Applies to tier: Power User, Admin
Business unit: all
Console page: /admin/audit-trail

The audit trail records every query, approval, decline, delivery and role change in the CDL, with the actor, time and scope.

## Query records
Each query record shows who ran it (a person in the console, or an agent via the MCP endpoint), the audience or run involved, the business units whose data was touched, the gating rules applied, and the outcome. Select a record to open its detail.

## Searching
Filter by actor, business unit, record type and date. Records are kept for 7 years and can't be edited or deleted.

## Exporting
Admins can export filtered records as CSV for compliance reviews.
