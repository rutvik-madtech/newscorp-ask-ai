# Request access to the console

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /request-access
Article type: how_to
Category: account

If you don't have an account, whether you work in a News Corp business unit or at an agency, ask for access on the **Request access** page. To get there, go to **Sign in** > **Request access**.

## Request access

1. On the **Sign in** page, click **Request access**. It sits under the **Sign in** button, on the right.
2. In **Full name**, type your name.
3. In **Work email**, type your work email address.
4. In **Comments for the approver**, give your business unit or agency, your role, and what you need access for. The field's hint reads "Your business unit or agency, role, and what you need access for".
5. Click **Send request**.

## What happens next

The page explains: "Access is approved manually by News Corp. You’ll get an email once it’s decided." News Corp reviews every request by hand, so access is never granted automatically. Watch the inbox of the work email you entered. The console has no status page where you can check a pending request.

## Good to know

- **Use your work email.** You later sign in with the work email your access was approved for, so enter that address in **Work email**.
- **Agency users use the same page.** If you are at an agency, name your agency in **Comments for the approver**. If you work in a News Corp business unit, name your business unit.
- **Roles.** The console's roles are **Power User**, for internal News Corp users, and **Agency**, for external agency users. Say which applies to you in **Comments for the approver**.
- **Already have access?** Click **Sign in** next to **Already have access?** to go back to the **Sign in** page.
- **Forgot your password instead?** If you already have an account but can't log in, use **Forgot password?** on the **Sign in** page rather than requesting access again.
