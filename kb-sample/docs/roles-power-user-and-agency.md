# Roles: Power User and Agency

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: All pages · sidebar role chip
Article type: concept
Category: getting_started

The console's roles are **Power User** and **Agency**. Your role decides which pages you see and what you can do. It shows as a chip under **Acting as** in the sidebar, with a short caption next to the role name.

## Power User

- Chip caption: **internal · can edit**.
- Role description: "Internal. Works as one business unit. Creates definitions, approves or declines requests for that unit, delivers."
- Chooses the business unit to work as in **Acting as**.
- Sees **Overview**, **Taxonomy**, **Audiences**, **Analysis**, **Rules of Use**, **Delivery** and the **Admin** section.

## Agency

- Chip caption: **external · read and size**. It means an external user who can browse audiences and see their sizes, then request them.
- Role description: "External. Finds, sizes and requests portfolio audiences. Never sees contributing properties or edits anything."
- **Acting as** is greyed out and set to **News Corp portfolio**.
- Sees only **Audiences**, which opens **Portfolio audiences**. The sidebar note reads "Overview, Taxonomy, Analysis, Rules of Use, Delivery and Admin are business-unit surfaces and are not rendered for your role."

## Admin is not a role

**Admin** is a sidebar section that Power Users see, holding **Access & roles** and **Connectors**. Nobody is given Admin as a role; the console's roles are **Power User** and **Agency**.

## What every role shares

- The header: the search box, **Ask AI** and the round account button with your initials. Ask AI works within what your role can see.
- The same sign-in pages: **Sign in**, **Reset your password** and **Request access**. The **Request access** form asks for "Your business unit or agency, role, and what you need access for".

## The roles are fixed

You can't add a role or rename one in the console.

## Why it matters

Your role explains why a page is missing from your sidebar. If you are an agency user and can't see **Analysis**, **Delivery** or another page a colleague mentions, it is because those are business-unit surfaces shown only to Power Users. If you are a Power User, every sidebar section is shown to you, and **Acting as** sets the business unit you work as.
