# Approve a request

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /rules-of-use
Article type: how_to
Category: rules_of_use

To approve a request, go to Sidebar > **Rules of Use**, find the request card and click **Review and approve**. The **Approve request** dialog shows exactly what you are authorising: “You are approving exactly this. Nothing else is authorised.” Figures in the dialog are as of the time stated at the top.

## Approve a request

1. In the sidebar, set **Acting as** to your business unit and click **Rules of Use**.
2. On the request card, click **Review and approve**. The **Approve request** dialog opens.
3. Review what you are approving:
   - **Requester**: who asked. An agency's buyer agent shows **(credentialed, MCP)**.
   - **Audience**: the audience, **pinned to** the version you are approving.
   - **Your fields used**: your unit's fields in the audience. **shape the cohort only** means they choose who is in the audience but are not sent.
   - **Fields that leave**: the data actually sent. **None · cohort membership only** means only who is in the audience is shared, with no field values.
   - **Destination**: where the audience goes and how it is delivered.
   - **Size · at request time**: the matched size when the request was made, with a note such as **no modelled extension**.
   - **Retention there**: how long the destination may keep the data.
4. Check the safety checks:
   - **Minimum population**: **Passed** means the audience meets the minimum population.
   - **Consent · confirmed at source**: **Passed** means consent was confirmed where the data came from.
   - **Source obfuscation**: **On** means which business unit each person came from is hidden.
   - **Raw PII outbound**: **Never** means no raw personal data is sent out.
5. Tick **I have checked the fields and destination above.**
6. Click **Approve**.

## Good to know

- Your approval covers exactly what the dialog shows: this requester, this audience version, these fields and this destination. Nothing else is authorised.
- Every decision is recorded with your name and the time.
- To back out, click **Cancel** or ×. You return to Rules of Use and the request stays pending.
- If you don't want to approve, close the dialog and click **Decline** on the card instead.
- A field marked **shape the cohort only** never travels as a field. What leaves is listed under **Fields that leave**.
- Requests that open on their own page, with the breadcrumb **Rules of Use / Request …** and an **Approve this delivery** button, are delivery requests. See Review a delivery request.
- Nothing leaves until a person in each business unit whose data is involved approves.
