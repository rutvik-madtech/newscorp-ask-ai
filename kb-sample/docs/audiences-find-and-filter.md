# Find and filter audience definitions

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: how_to
Category: audiences

Your audiences are on the **Audiences** page: Sidebar > **Audiences**. The page subtitle reads "Each definition is evaluated against every contributing unit’s own data, in place." A table lists every audience definition, and you can narrow it with **Filters**.

## See your audiences

1. In the sidebar, click **Audiences**. You can also click the **All …** link beside the **Largest definitions** heading on the Overview.
2. Read the table. Its columns are **Definition**, **Type**, **Owner**, **Matched**, **Modelled**, **Precedence** and **Status**.
3. Check the summary line above the table, **Showing … of … definitions · sizes as of …**. It tells you how many definitions match your filters out of the total, and the time the sizes were calculated, in UTC.
4. To open a definition, click its name in the **Definition** column. Its detail panel opens on the right of the same page.

## Filter the list

1. Click **Filters**.
2. Active filters show as chips next to the button, such as **Type:** and **Owner:**. Use the **Owner:** filter to limit the list by owning business unit. The value **Any** means that filter puts no limit on the list.
3. To remove one filter, click the × on its chip.
4. To clear every filter at once, click **Clear all**.

The summary line updates to show how many definitions match the filters you have set.

## Good to know

- Sizes are a snapshot. The summary line shows when they were calculated, and an open definition shows the same under **Size · as of …**.
- The footnote under the table reads: "Matched people are consented and deduplicated on the spine. Modelled extensions are separate audiences and never summed with matched." That is why **Matched** and **Modelled** sit in separate columns.
- To start a new audience, use the buttons at the top of the page: **New audience** opens Create an audience, and **From a seed** starts an audience from an advertiser's seed file.
- To look for a definition by name, you can also use the search box in the header.
- This list is for Power Users. With the Agency role, **Audiences** opens Portfolio audiences instead.
