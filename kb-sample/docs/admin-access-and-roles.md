# View people and roles

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /admin/access-roles
Article type: how_to
Category: admin

To see who has access to the console and what each role can do, go to Sidebar > Admin > **Access & roles**. The page heading reads **Access and roles** and the breadcrumb reads Admin / Access and roles. The page has a **Roles** panel at the top and a **People** table below it.

## See what each role can do

1. In the sidebar, under **Admin**, click **Access & roles**.
2. Read the **Roles** panel. It has a card for each role:
   - **Power User**: "Internal. Works as one business unit. Creates definitions, approves or declines requests for that unit, delivers."
   - **Agency**: "External. Finds, sizes and requests portfolio audiences. Never sees contributing properties or edits anything."

## See who has access

1. On **Access and roles**, scroll to **People**.
2. Read the table. Each row is one person with access:
   - **Person**: the person.
   - **Business unit**: the News Corp business unit they work in, such as Dow Jones or Realtor.com. Agency people show **Agency** here.
   - **Role**: a **Power User** chip (blue) or an **Agency** chip (grey).
   - **Last active (UTC)**: when the person last used the console, in UTC.
3. At the right of each row is a **Disable** button.

## Check when someone last used the console

1. Go to Sidebar > Admin > **Access & roles**.
2. In **People**, find the person's row.
3. Read the **Last active (UTC)** column.

## Good to know

- **The roles are fixed.** A chip in the **Roles** panel header says so, and the page has no control to create, rename or edit a role.
- **Role and business unit are read-only.** The **People** table shows **Role** and **Business unit** but offers no control to change them.
- **Admin is not a role.** **Admin** is a sidebar section for Power Users that holds **Access & roles** and **Connectors**. The Agency role doesn't see the Admin section.
- **Adding or removing people.** To add a user, click **Invite person** in the **People** section header. To remove someone's access, click **Disable** on their row. See Invite a person or disable access.
