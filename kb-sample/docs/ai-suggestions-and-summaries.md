# Work with AI suggestions and AI summaries

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: how_to
Category: ask_ai

The console marks anything the AI wrote with a purple dashed border and a tag, so you can tell it apart from records and from what people entered. You accept, edit or dismiss AI suggestions on Sidebar > Audiences > New audience, rate AI summaries on Rules of Use and on a run's page under Analysis, and edit an AI-drafted decline message before you send it.

## How AI content is marked

- **AI suggestion**: something the AI proposes for you to use, such as a membership rule.
- **AI summary**: a plain-language summary the AI wrote about what is on the page.
- **AI draft · edit before sending**: a message the AI drafted for you to check and edit.

## Accept, edit or dismiss an AI suggestion

1. Go to Sidebar > **Audiences** > **New audience**, and under **Step 1 · Start from** choose **An analysis run**.
2. Under **Step 2 · Membership rule**, find the box titled **Suggested rule from run …** with the **AI suggestion** tag.
3. Open **Why this?** to read the reasons behind the suggestion.
4. Click **Accept into rule** to use the rule as it is, **Edit first** to change it before you use it, or **Dismiss** to reject it.

Elsewhere, an AI suggestion has **Accept** (use it as is), **Edit** (change it first), **Dismiss** (discard it) and **Why this?** (show the reasons).

## Read and rate an AI summary

1. Find the card tagged **AI summary**:
   - **In plain terms**, on request cards that have it under Sidebar > **Rules of Use**. It explains the request in plain language and how your gating rules apply.
   - **What this run shows**, on a run's detail page (Sidebar > **Analysis** > **Runs** > click the run's name). It summarises the run's results.
2. Read the **Based on:** line to see which sources the summary used. On a run, it names the composition table on that page and that run only.
3. Next to **Was this right?**, click **Helpful**, **Wrong facts** if the summary gets something wrong, or **Outdated** if it is out of date.

## Edit an AI-drafted decline message

1. Go to Sidebar > **Rules of Use** and click **Decline** on the request card.
2. Under **Reason**, pick the reason that fits.
3. Under **Message sent to the requester**, read the draft tagged **AI draft · edit before sending** and edit it as needed. It is drafted from your gating rule and the reason you picked.
4. Click **Decline request** when the message is right.

## Good to know

- The line under the decline draft reads "Nothing is sent until you press Decline."
- **AI unavailable** (a grey chip) means the AI feature can't be used right now. Carry on without it.
