# Overview and navigation FAQ

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /
Article type: faq
Category: overview

Quick answers about the **Overview** page (Sidebar > **Overview**) and about moving around the console as a Power User.

**Can I approve or decline requests from the Overview page?**
No. The Overview shows what is waiting for you, but you decide on Rules of Use. Click **Open Rules of Use** on the **Awaiting your approval** card, or **Open all** on the **Waiting on you** panel, then approve or decline there.

**Why does the Overview only show one business unit, such as Dow Jones?**
The Overview shows the business unit selected in **Acting as** at the top of the sidebar, and its subtitle names that unit. To see another unit, switch **Acting as** to it.

**What does Pending spine mean?**
It appears under the **Portfolio reachable · matched** card. The portfolio-wide deduplicated figure is not available, so a placeholder shows instead of a value.

**What do the badges in the sidebar mean?**
The amber number on **Rules of Use** counts the requests awaiting you. A red dot with a number on **Connectors** means a connector needs attention; click **Connectors** to check connector health.

**What does internal · can edit mean?**
It sits beside your role chip, **Power User**, under **Acting as**. It means you are an internal Power User who can make changes.

**What does the search box search?**
The search box at the top of every page reads "Search definitions, runs, query ids". Type a definition name, a run or a query id to look for it.

**Why is an item in Waiting on you red?**
A red **Expires in …** chip means the request is close to expiring, so deal with it first. A grey **Received …** chip only shows when the request arrived.

**How do I get back to a list from a detail page?**
Click the section name at the start of the breadcrumb above the page heading, such as **Audiences** in **Audiences / New**. You can also click the section in the sidebar.
