# Ask AI: open the panel and ask a question

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: Header > Ask AI (all pages)
Article type: how_to
Category: ask_ai

**Ask AI** is the help assistant in the header of every console page, to the right of the search box and next to your account button. Click it (Header > **Ask AI**) to open the **Ask AI · about this page** panel, then pick a suggested question or type your own question about the page you are on.

## Open Ask AI and ask a question

1. Go to the page you have a question about.
2. In the header, click **Ask AI**. The **Ask AI · about this page** panel opens on the right, and the page stays visible on the left.
3. Check what Ask AI covers for you. Power Users: the box at the top of the panel lists **Sees:**, **Cannot see:** and **Cannot do:**. Agency users: Ask AI sees only what your role can see.
4. Ask your question:
   - Under **Try asking**, click one of the suggested questions, or
   - Click the **Ask about this page** box at the bottom of the panel, type your own question in plain words and click **Send**.
5. Read the reply. Your question appears on the right of the panel, and the answer appears below it, marked with the **AI answer** chip.
6. To make the panel bigger, click **Expand panel** (⤢) at the top right of the panel.
7. To close the panel, click **×** (**Close Ask AI**) at the top right. You go back to the page you were on, without the panel.

## Good to know

- **Ask AI** is in the header for Power Users and agency users alike, so you can open it from any page you can see.
- Ask AI helps with the page you are on. The panel heading reads **Ask AI · about this page**, and the **Try asking** suggestions are questions about that page.
- You don't have to use a suggestion. Type your own question, or a follow-up, in **Ask about this page** and click **Send**.
- Ask AI is read-only. The line under the question box reads "Answers never change data." Asking a question never changes anything in the console.
- Each answer shows what it is based on and how current it is, so you can check it before you rely on it.
- If you are a Power User, the scope box follows your role and the business unit selected in **Acting as**.
