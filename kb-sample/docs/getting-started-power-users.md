# Getting started for Power Users

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /
Article type: getting_started
Category: getting_started

As a Power User, you use the News Corp Data Dexterity Layer console to define audiences, analyse customer lists, approve or decline uses of your business unit's data and deliver audiences, with every unit's own rules applied. Start at Sidebar > **Overview**.

## Your role

The chip under **Acting as** at the top of the sidebar reads **Power User** with **internal · can edit**. The **Roles** panel on Admin > **Access & roles** describes it as "Internal. Works as one business unit. Creates definitions, approves or declines requests for that unit, delivers." You work as one business unit at a time, chosen in the **Acting as** dropdown. The Overview subtitle names the unit you are acting as.

## A tour of the sidebar

- **Overview**: what needs your decision today, then the health of everything your unit contributes.
- **Taxonomy**: the shared vocabulary that sits above each unit's own taxonomy.
- **Audiences**: audience definitions, **New audience** and **From a seed** (match an advertiser list).
- **Analysis**: analysis runs on the **Runs** tab and portfolio-wide questions on the **Portfolio** tab.
- **Rules of Use**: approve or decline requests to use your unit's fields. An amber badge shows requests awaiting you.
- **Delivery**: send audiences and see what has already been sent.
- **Admin** section: **Access & roles** (people and roles) and **Connectors** (the feeds the layer depends on). A red dot on **Connectors** means a connector needs attention.

## The header

Every page has a search box ("Search definitions, runs, query ids"), the **Ask AI** button, which opens a panel about the page you are on, and the round account button with your initials.

## First things to do, and to check each day

1. Check that **Acting as** shows the business unit you want to work as.
2. On **Overview**, read **Awaiting your approval**: how many requests are waiting and when the oldest expires. **Waiting on you** lists each request with an **Expires in …** or **Received …** chip.
3. Click **Open Rules of Use** to approve or decline anything waiting. You decide there, not on the Overview.
4. Browse **Audiences**, or click a definition in **Largest definitions** on the Overview to open it.
5. Look for a red dot on **Connectors** under **Admin**.

## Principles you will see everywhere

- Matched and modelled figures are never added together. Modelled extensions sit in their own column, such as **Modelled · separate**.
- Groups below the minimum population are suppressed: they show **Suppressed** and no value.
- Row-level data and raw personal data never leave the layer.
- Nothing leaves the layer until a person in each business unit whose data is involved approves. An expired request lapses and nothing is delivered.
