# Audiences FAQ

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: faq
Category: audiences

Answers to common questions about Sidebar > **Audiences**, where you find, create and deliver audience definitions.

**How do I edit or delete an audience definition?**
Go to Sidebar > **Audiences** and click the definition's name in the **Definition** column. The detail panel on the right offers **Duplicate** and **Deliver**. It shows no edit or delete button.

**Where are my drafts after Save as draft?**
**Save as draft** keeps the definition without delivering it or sending it for approval. Sidebar > **Audiences** lists your definitions, each with its **Status**. **Filters** narrow the list, and applied filters show as chips such as **Type:** and **Owner:**.

**Who has to approve my cross-unit audience?**
Each other business unit whose data is used. **Gates** shows an approval gate named after that unit, such as **Realtor.com approval**, and a person in that unit approves or declines in Rules of Use. Nothing leaves until a person in each business unit whose data is involved approves.

**Why does my audience show Pending you?**
**Pending you** in the **Status** column means the definition is waiting for action from the business unit you are acting as, which you set in **Acting as**.

**Why can't I add the modelled number to matched?**
They are separate audiences. The note under the table reads "Matched people are consented and deduplicated on the spine. Modelled extensions are separate audiences and never summed with matched." They are also delivered as separately labelled audiences.

**What does evaluated in place mean?**
The page subtitle reads "Each definition is evaluated against every contributing unit’s own data, in place." Each definition runs against each business unit's own data where it sits.

**Is the advertiser's file part of the audience?**
No. "The advertiser’s file is not part of the audience." Only membership of an approved audience leaves, never the advertiser's file.

**How fresh are the sizes?**
Sizes are as of the time on the summary line above the table, **Showing … of … definitions · sizes as of …**, in UTC. The detail panel shows **Size · as of …**. On **Create an audience**, **Live sizing** "Updates as you edit" and shows when it was last evaluated.

**Can agency users create audiences?**
No. The Agency role sees only **Audiences**, which opens **Portfolio audiences**, where agency users find, size and request portfolio audiences. They can't create or edit definitions.
