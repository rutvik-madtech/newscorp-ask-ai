# Use the Portfolio tab

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /analysis
Article type: how_to
Category: analysis

The **Portfolio** tab on Sidebar > **Analysis** shows what the whole portfolio can reach and where segments from different business units overlap. Its subtitle reads: "Portfolio-wide answers with no advertiser file involved." It is also where you ask a portfolio question.

## Read the Portfolio tab

1. In the sidebar, click **Analysis**, then click the **Portfolio** tab next to **Runs**.
2. At the top, the **Ask a portfolio question** card holds the **Your question, in plain language** box and the **Resolve terms** button. "Nothing runs until you have seen the terms."
3. To change the period, open the **Period** dropdown and choose a period. The **As of … UTC** text next to it shows when the figures were refreshed.
4. Read **What the portfolio can reach**. Each row shows the **Business unit**, the **Audience** and its **Reach · matched**. A row below the minimum population shows **Suppressed** (with a lock icon) and no value: "Suppressed rows show no value at all: they fall below the minimum population."
5. Read **Where two units meet**. Each row has:
   - **Segment pair**: a segment from each unit in the pair.
   - **Minimum size**: **Passed** (green) when the pair meets the minimum population, or **Below** (amber) when it falls below it.
   - **Overlap**: **Not verified** until the analysis is verified against the spine. A pair whose **Minimum size** reads **Below** shows a dash (—).

## Good to know

- Why no overlap percentage? The note under the table reads: "No overlap percentage is shown until the analysis is verified against the spine."
- **Below** in **Minimum size** means the pair falls below the minimum population, so its **Overlap** shows a dash and no figure.
- The table shows the pairs, their **Minimum size** and their **Overlap**. It has no control to verify a pair or add one.
- **Reach · matched** counts matched people only.
- The **Portfolio** tab has no **New analysis** button. To upload a customer list, go to the **Runs** tab.
- If you open **Ask AI** on this tab, its scope box lists overlap % under **Cannot see:**.
