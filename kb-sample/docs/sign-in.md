# Sign in to the console

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /login
Article type: how_to
Category: account

You sign in (log in) on the **Sign in** page of the News Corp Data Dexterity Layer console, the page whose logo reads "powered by MadConnect". You need approved access before you can sign in, and you always sign in with the work email that access was approved for.

## Sign in

1. Open the console. The **Sign in** page is the entry page you see before you are signed in.
2. In **Email**, type your work email. The page reminds you: "Use the work email your access was approved for."
3. In **Password**, type your password.
4. Click **Sign in**.

## Where you land after signing in

- If you are a Power User, you start on **Overview**.
- If you are an agency user, you work from **Audiences**, which opens **Portfolio audiences** and is the only item in your sidebar.

## Links under the Sign in button

- **Forgot password?**, on the left, opens the **Reset your password** page, where you ask for an emailed link to set a new password.
- **Request access**, on the right, opens the **Request access** page, where you ask News Corp for access to the console.

## Good to know

- The **Sign in** page asks only for your work email in **Email** and your password in **Password**. It shows no other sign-in option.
- Use the work email your access was approved for, not a personal address or another work address.
- If you don't have access, click **Request access**. Access is approved manually by News Corp.
- You also come back to the **Sign in** page from **Back to sign in** on **Reset your password**, and from **Sign in** next to **Already have access?** on **Request access**.
- If you are stuck on the **Sign in** page, check the email you typed, reset your password with **Forgot password?**, or request access if you were never approved.
