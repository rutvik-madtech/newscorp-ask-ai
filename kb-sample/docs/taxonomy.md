# Taxonomy: canonical and local terms

Applies to tier: Power User, Admin, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /taxonomy

The Taxonomy page is the shared vocabulary every audience is built from. Each term describes a kind of person, such as "Small-business owners" or "High-net-worth households", and says how the CDL decides who belongs.

## Canonical and local terms
- **Canonical terms** follow the IAB Audience Taxonomy 1.1. They mean the same thing in every business unit, so a Cross-BU audience can combine them safely.
- **Local terms** are defined by one business unit for its own data. They are listed under "Local · not modified" until they are mapped to a canonical term.

## Reading a term
A term's detail panel shows its definition, the sources that contribute to it (for example "Dow Jones · declared" or "Realtor.com · behavioural"), whether membership is matched, modelled or definitional, and its version history.

## Versions
Changing a term's definition creates a new version, for example raising the employee-count floor for "Small-business owners" to 50. Audiences stay pinned to the version that produced them, so measurement that comes back later can be traced to the exact definition. To use a newer definition, duplicate the audience; the copy uses the latest version.

## Proposing a change
Power Users can propose a new local term, or a change to an existing term, from the term's detail panel. A change to a canonical term needs agreement from every business unit that uses it.
