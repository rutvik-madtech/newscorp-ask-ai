# Term versions and comparing them

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /taxonomy
Article type: how_to
Category: taxonomy

Each change to a canonical term or to how a business unit maps to it is kept as a version. Go to Sidebar > **Taxonomy**, open the term, and read **Version history** to see what changed, or click **Compare versions** to compare.

## See which version you are viewing

1. In the sidebar, click **Taxonomy**, then click the term in the canonical list.
2. Look at the chip next to the term name. It shows the version number and **current**, which means you are viewing the term's current version.

## See what changed in a term

1. Open the term.
2. Scroll to **Version history**. Each version is listed with a short note of what changed and the date it changed.

Changes to the term itself and changes to a business unit's mapping both show here as new versions.

## Compare versions

1. Open the term.
2. Click **Compare versions** at the top right of the term, next to **Use in an audience**.

## Good to know

- Delivered audiences don't change when a term changes. The note under **Version history** reads "Delivered audiences stay pinned to the version that produced them, so returned measurement stays traceable."
- Approvals are pinned to one audience version too. In Sidebar > **Rules of Use** > **Review and approve**, the **Approve request …** dialog shows the **Audience** followed by **pinned to …** with the version being approved. The dialog states "You are approving exactly this. Nothing else is authorised."
- Ask AI can explain what changed between versions of an audience definition. On **Audiences**, the panel's **Sees:** line includes the definition's versions, and **Try asking** offers a question about what changed between versions. An answer can list a term's history under **Based on:**, and that link opens Taxonomy.
- Ask AI is read-only: "Answers never change data."
