# Find a term and read its details

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /taxonomy
Article type: how_to
Category: taxonomy

To look up a shared term, go to Sidebar > **Taxonomy**, search with **Find a term** or browse the canonical categories, and click the term. Its details open on the right of the same page, where you can also start an audience from it.

## Find a term

1. In the sidebar, click **Taxonomy**.
2. Type in **Find a term** at the top of the left panel to search, or browse the canonical list grouped under **Demographics**, **Interest**, **Intent** and **Behaviour**.
3. Click the term. It is highlighted in the list and its details open on the right.

## Read the term's details

At the top of the detail panel you see:

- The term name, with a version chip marked **current**, and the term's definition underneath.
- **Steward**: the business unit responsible for the term.
- **Contributing units**: how many business units are mapped to the term, out of all units, written as "… of … mapped".
- **Portfolio · matched**: the term's matched size across the portfolio.
- **In use by**: how many audiences use the term, written as "… audiences".

Further down are **How each business unit maps to it** and **Version history**.

## Build an audience from a term

1. Open the term as above.
2. Click **Use in an audience** at the top right of the term.
3. The **Create an audience** page opens, with the breadcrumb **Audiences / New**. Under **Step 1 · Start from**, **A blank definition** lets you "Write a rule from the shared vocabulary".

## Good to know

- The note under the fields reads **Size as of … · use count live**. The **Portfolio · matched** size is a snapshot as of the time shown, in UTC. The **In use by** count is live.
- **Compare versions**, next to **Use in an audience**, compares the term's versions.
- **Contributing units** is a summary. To see which units are mapped and which show **No equivalent**, read **How each business unit maps to it**.
- Agency users don't see the Taxonomy page.
