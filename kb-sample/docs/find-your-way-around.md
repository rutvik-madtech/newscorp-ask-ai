# Find your way around the console

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /
Article type: how_to
Category: overview

Every page in the console shares the same frame: a sidebar on the left for moving between areas, and a header across the top with the search box, **Ask AI** and your account button. On pages inside a section, a breadcrumb above the page heading takes you back to the list.

## Get around the console

1. **Read the sidebar from the top.** You see the News Corp Data Dexterity Layer logo, then the **Acting as** dropdown (the business unit you are working for), then your role chip, **Power User**, with **internal · can edit** beside it.
2. **Go to a page.** Click an item in the main navigation: **Overview**, **Taxonomy**, **Audiences**, **Analysis**, **Rules of Use** or **Delivery**. Below them, the **Admin** section holds **Access & roles** and **Connectors**. The page you are on is highlighted.
3. **Check the badges.** An amber number on **Rules of Use** counts the requests awaiting you. A red dot with a number on **Connectors** means a connector needs attention; click **Connectors** to check connector health.
4. **Search.** Click the search box at the top of the page. It reads "Search definitions, runs, query ids". Type a definition name, a run or a query id.
5. **Ask a question.** Click **Ask AI** on the right of the header. The **Ask AI · about this page** panel opens beside the page you are on.
6. **Find your account.** The round button with your initials, at the top right of the header, is your account button.
7. **Go back to a list.** On a detail page, the breadcrumb above the heading shows where you are, for example **Audiences / New** on Create an audience, **Analysis / Run …** on an analysis run, or **Rules of Use / Request …** on a request. Click the section name at the start, such as **Audiences**, to return to its list.

## Good to know

- You can also return to a list from its sidebar item. For example, click **Audiences** in the sidebar to get back to the audience list.
- On Admin pages the breadcrumb starts with **Admin**, for example **Admin / Connectors** or **Admin / Access and roles**. **Admin** is a section name, not a link. On a connector's own page, click **Connectors** in the breadcrumb to go back to the connector list.
- The sidebar item reads **Access & roles** and the page heading reads **Access and roles**. They are the same page.
- **Acting as** decides which business unit's work you see. To change it, see Switch the business unit you're acting as.
- Ask AI only answers questions. The panel states: "Answers never change data."
