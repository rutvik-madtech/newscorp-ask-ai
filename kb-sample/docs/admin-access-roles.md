# Manage users and roles

Applies to tier: Admin
Business unit: all
Console page: /admin/access-roles

Admins manage who can use the console, and what they can see, in **Admin › Access & roles**.

## Add a user
1. Select **Add user**.
2. Enter the person's work email and name.
3. Choose a role: Power User, Admin or Agency.
4. Choose the business units they may work in. Agency users also need an agency name, which decides which shared audiences they see.
5. Select **Invite**. The user receives an email to set a password.

## Change a role or business units
Open the user, change the role or business units, and select **Save**. A role change applies at the user's next sign-in. Removing a business unit hides its audiences from the user straight away.

## Deactivate a user
Open the user and select **Deactivate**. Their runs and deliveries stay in the audit trail.

Every role change is written to the audit trail.
