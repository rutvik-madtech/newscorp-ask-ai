# Read seed match results

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: how_to
Category: audiences

After you click **Match against the portfolio** on From a seed, the page heading reads “Matched, before anything is extended” (breadcrumb **Audiences / From a seed / Matched**). The page shows how many people in the advertiser's seed the portfolio found, where they were found and how the match ran. **Match** is highlighted in **Progress**.

## Read the results

1. Read the **Matched · deterministic** card. It shows the matched figure, its share **of seed** and when the match ran, with the line “Real people the portfolio can reach today.” This is how many of the advertiser's customers you matched.
2. Next to it, **Modelled · not yet offered** shows a dash (—) until you extend. “Extension is a separate step, from a separate provider, reported as its own number.”
3. Read **Where they were found**. The **Where matched** and **People** columns break the matched figure down:
   - **Dow Jones only**
   - **Realtor.com only**
   - **Both · counted once**: people found in Dow Jones and in Realtor.com, counted only once.
4. Read **How the match ran**:
   - **Method**: **Deterministic, on the licensed spine**
   - **Ran where**: **Inside the News Corp account**
   - **Eligibility**: **Applied at each unit before matching**
   - **Unmatched seed rows**: **Discarded, never stored**
   - **Minimum population**: **Passed**
5. To go further, click **Look at extension**.

## Good to know

- The page says “This is the number the advertiser is given first” and adds: “Extension is optional, priced separately and never added into the figure above.”
- Does the advertiser see which unit a person came from? No. “The advertiser sees this breakdown. It is not told which unit any individual came from.”
- Seed rows that don't match are discarded and never stored.
- Matching is deterministic: an exact match on the identifier against the licensed spine, not a model or an estimate.
- Each business unit applies its eligibility before matching.
- **Minimum population** shows **Passed** when the matched audience meets the minimum population.
