# Glossary: console, roles and approvals

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: All pages
Article type: reference
Category: concepts

This glossary explains the console, role and approval terms you meet on every page, from the sidebar to **Rules of Use** and **Delivery**. Terms are in alphabetical order.

- **Acting as**: the business unit you work on behalf of, chosen in the dropdown at the top of the sidebar.
- **Admin**: the sidebar section that holds **Access & roles** and **Connectors**. It is a section Power Users see, not a role.
- **Agent (agency agent)**: an agency's automated agent that sends requests to the layer through MCP. Its requests carry the chip **Agent request · MCP** in Rules of Use.
- **Ask AI**: the read-only help panel you open from **Ask AI** in the header. "Answers never change data."
- **Audience definition**: a saved, rule-based audience, listed under **Audiences**.
- **Business unit**: a News Corp unit that contributes data, such as Dow Jones or Realtor.com.
- **Canonical term / shared vocabulary**: the common terms every business unit maps its own data to, listed on **Taxonomy**. Each unit's own taxonomy stays unchanged.
- **Connector**: a feed or file exchange the layer depends on. Admin > **Connectors** shows each one's health.
- **Data steward**: a job function shown next to the requester's business unit on requests in Rules of Use. It is not a console role.
- **Destination / destination class**: where an audience goes. The destination classes are **Your own lake / S3**, **Activation**, **Curation** and **External clean room**, shown as cards on Delivery.
- **Gate**: a check an audience or request must pass, such as **Minimum population** or an approval gate named after a business unit, for example **Realtor.com approval**.
- **Gating rules**: a business unit's **DENY** and **ALLOW** rules for its fields, under Rules of Use > **Your gating rules**.
- **MCP**: the credentialed connection agency agents use to send requests to the layer.
- **News Corp Data Dexterity Layer (the layer)**: the platform the console runs on.
- **Permissible-use envelope**: the usage rules that travel with every delivery, shown on Delivery.
- **Portfolio**: the News Corp business units taken together.
- **Power User / Agency**: the console's roles. Power User is the internal role (**internal · can edit**) and Agency is the external role (**external · read and size**).
- **Precedence**: which business unit's value wins when units disagree. You set it in **Precedence when units disagree** on Create an audience.
- **Rules of Use**: the page where a business unit approves or declines uses of its data. Nothing enters a cross-unit output until a person in each unit whose data is involved approves.
