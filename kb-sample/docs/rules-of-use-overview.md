# Rules of Use: how approvals work

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /rules-of-use
Article type: concept
Category: rules_of_use

Rules of Use is where your business unit approves or declines requests to use its fields in an output that leaves the unit. Open it from Sidebar > **Rules of Use**. An amber badge on the sidebar item counts the requests awaiting you, and the page title reads **Rules of Use ·** followed by the business unit selected in **Acting as**.

## What it is

The page subtitle sums it up: “Nothing of yours enters a cross-unit output until a person here approves it. Every decision is recorded with the approver’s name and the time.”

Who approves use of your data? Your unit does. The decision sits with the business unit whose fields are used, not with the requester. If another unit or an agency's buyer agent wants to use your fields, a person in your unit must approve before anything is delivered. The page lists requests for the unit you are acting as only, so switch **Acting as** to see another unit's requests.

## Kinds of request

The **Request type** tabs are **All**, **Agent**, **Cross-BU** and **Destination**. Each request card carries a chip:

- **Agent request · MCP**: an agency's buyer agent sent the request through MCP.
- **Cross-BU output**: another business unit's output uses your fields.
- **New destination**: an audience already approved for one destination class asks to go to another.

## Where requests come from

A request arrives when someone:

- clicks **Send for approval** on **Create an audience** or **From a seed**
- clicks the **Send variant … for approval** button when comparing variants, which names the variant.

A request also arrives when an agency's buyer agent sends one through MCP.

## What's on the page

- Request cards: one per request, each with **Review and approve** and **Decline**.
- **Your gating rules**: your unit's **DENY** and **ALLOW** rules, “Evaluated deny-first, top to bottom.”
- **Recent decisions**: where you see past decisions. Each line shows **Approved** or **Declined**, the audience and its destination, and the date.

You can also get here from the Overview: click **Open Rules of Use** on the **Awaiting your approval** card, or **Open all** in **Waiting on you**. You approve or decline on Rules of Use, not on the Overview.

## Why it matters

Rules of Use keeps your unit in control of its own data:

- Nothing leaves until a person in each business unit whose data is involved approves.
- Row-level data and raw PII never leave.
- An expired request lapses and nothing is delivered. Requests never approve themselves.
- Every approve or decline is recorded with the name of the person who decided and the time.

Rules of Use is not shown to the Agency role. Agent requests come from an agency's buyer agent, which sends them through MCP.
