# When a request expires

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /rules-of-use
Article type: concept
Category: rules_of_use

Every approval request has a deadline. If nobody in the business unit whose data is used decides before it runs out, the request lapses and nothing is delivered. You see the time left on Sidebar > **Rules of Use**, on the **Overview** and on an audience that is waiting for approval.

## What expiry means

A delivery request states the rule: “Nothing is delivered on a timeout, and an expired request lapses rather than passing.” A request never approves itself. If no one decides in time, it ends without approval and the audience does not go to the destination. Nothing leaves until a person in each business unit whose data is involved approves.

How long do you have to approve a request? Until the time shown runs out, where a request shows one: **expires in …** on an **Agent request · MCP** card, or the **Expires in …** chip on a delivery request. The **Overview** shows **Oldest expires in …** for your oldest request.

## Where you see the deadline

- **Rules of Use request cards**: on an **Agent request · MCP** card, the requester line shows when the request arrived, then **expires in …**.
- **A delivery request**: open the request from Rules of Use (the breadcrumb reads **Rules of Use / Request …**). A red **Expires in …** chip sits at the top right.
- **Overview**: the **Awaiting your approval** card shows **Oldest expires in …**, the time left on your oldest request. In **Waiting on you**, a red **Expires in …** chip marks a request close to expiring, and a grey **Received …** chip shows when other requests arrived.
- **From a seed**: after you send an audience for approval, the **Deliver to …** button (it names the destination) stays locked. The text under it reads “Unlocks when … approves. Request sent … · expires in …”, so you can see how long the other unit has to decide.

## Why it matters

- Act on the oldest requests first. Start with **Oldest expires in …** and any red **Expires in …** chips, then click **Open Rules of Use** or **Open all** to reach the requests.
- Letting a request expire is not the same as approving it. To let a use go ahead, approve it before the deadline: click **Review and approve** on the request card, or **Approve this delivery** on a delivery request.
- To stop a use, click **Decline**. “Declining stops this delivery and is recorded with your reason.”
- Why did my approval request lapse? Nobody in the business unit whose data is used approved it before the time shown ran out. It lapsed rather than passing, and nothing was delivered.
