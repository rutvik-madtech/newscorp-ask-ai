# Portfolio audiences FAQ

Applies to tier: Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: faq
Category: portfolio_audiences

Answers to common questions from agency users about **Portfolio audiences**, the page you open from Sidebar > **Audiences** to find, size and request audiences.

**Can I create my own audience or edit a definition?**
No. The box under the table reads "Not available to your role: creating or editing definitions, setting precedence, changing the taxonomy, or sending to a business unit’s own systems." You can find, size and request the audiences listed on Portfolio audiences.

**Why do I only see Audiences in the sidebar?**
The other areas are for business units. The note under **Audiences** reads "Overview, Taxonomy, Analysis, Rules of Use, Delivery and Admin are business-unit surfaces and are not rendered for your role."

**Why is Acting as greyed out?**
For agency users, **Acting as** is locked to **News Corp portfolio**, so you can't switch to a single business unit. You see audiences across the News Corp portfolio.

**Can I see which News Corp properties contribute to an audience?**
No. Contributing properties are not named. **Sources** in the detail panel shows how many News Corp properties contribute.

**Who approves my request, and is anything delivered before that?**
Your request goes to each contributing business unit, and a person there approves or declines it. Nothing is delivered before that.

**Where do I choose where my audience is sent?**
Check **Available on** for the audience before you request it; it shows where the audience can be used. The detail panel offers **Request this audience** and says "On approval the audience arrives at your chosen destination."

**I requested an audience but nothing has arrived. How do I check on it?**
Portfolio audiences shows **What happens when you request** but no list of your requests or their status. Nothing arrives until a person at each contributing business unit approves your request.

**Can I export a list of the people in an audience?**
No. Sizes are aggregate, totals only, and no individual records are shared. Portfolio audiences shows sizes and offers **Request this audience**.

**What does external · read and size mean?**
It is the caption next to your **Agency** role chip in the sidebar. You are an external agency user, and you can browse and size audiences on Portfolio audiences and request them.
