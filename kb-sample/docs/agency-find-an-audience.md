# Find a portfolio audience

Applies to tier: Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: how_to
Category: portfolio_audiences

The list of audiences available to you, the ones you can size and request, is on **Portfolio audiences**: Sidebar > **Audiences**. The page subtitle reads "Find an audience, size it, and request it. Contributing properties are not named."

## Find an audience

1. In the sidebar, click **Audiences**. It opens **Portfolio audiences**. For agency users, **Audiences** is the only item in the sidebar.
2. To filter the list by category, click a **Category** tab above the table: **All**, **Demographics**, **Intent** or **Behaviour**. **All** is selected when the page opens and shows every audience.
3. Scan the table. Its columns are:
   - **Audience**: the audience name, with a short description underneath.
   - **Label**: how the audience was built, for example **Matched**.
   - **Reach**: how big the audience is.
   - **Available on**: where the audience can be used, such as **Clean room**, **Clean room · activation** or **Insights only**.
4. Check the note under the table, **Reach as of …**. It shows when the reach figures were last refreshed, in UTC.
5. Click a row to open that audience's details in the panel on the right.

## Good to know

- The **Reach** column shows the audience size. Sizes are aggregate: a total, never individual records.
- Sizes are minimum-population gated, so a size is shown only when the audience meets a minimum population.
- The **Reach** figure is the same size you see on the **Matched · deterministic** card in the detail panel.
- The **Category** tabs are the only filter on the page itself. The search box in the header is also available while you browse.
- Contributing properties are not named anywhere on the page.
- When you have found the audience you want, check **Available on**, then use **Request this audience** in the detail panel.
