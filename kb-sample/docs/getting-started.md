# Getting started with the CDL console

Applies to tier: Power User, Admin, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /

The CDL console is the web interface for the Composable Data Layer (CDL). News Corp business units use it to build audiences, analyse customer files and deliver audiences to approved destinations, under rules each business unit controls.

## Signing in
Sign in with your work email and password. The console keeps you signed in for the session. If your session expires, you are sent to the sign-in page and returned to the page you were on after you sign in again.

## Choosing a business unit
The business unit selector in the header sets which business unit you are working for, such as Dow Jones or Realtor.com. Audiences, analysis runs and deliveries are listed for the selected business unit. You only see the business units you have been given access to. To work in another business unit, ask an Admin.

## Finding your way around
The sidebar has six main pages:
- **Overview**: portfolio figures for the selected business unit.
- **Taxonomy**: the shared audience vocabulary, with canonical and local terms.
- **Audiences**: build audiences, check their gates and send them for approval.
- **Analysis**: upload a customer file and see who it matches and what they look like.
- **Rules of Use**: approval requests and the gating rules that decide what can leave the CDL.
- **Delivery**: send an approved audience to a destination and track the delivery.

Admins also see an **Admin** section with Access & roles, Consent ledger, Connectors and Audit trail. Not every page is available to every role; see "What each role can see".

## Getting help
Select **Ask AI** in the header to ask how the console works. Ask AI answers from the product documentation and links to the page it used. It can't see your audiences, files or live figures.
