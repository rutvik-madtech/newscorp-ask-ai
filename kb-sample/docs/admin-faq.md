# Admin FAQ

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /admin
Article type: faq
Category: admin

Answers to common questions about the Admin section of the sidebar: Sidebar > Admin > **Access & roles** for people and roles, and Sidebar > Admin > **Connectors** for feeds and file exchanges.

**Is Admin a role?**
No. **Admin** is a sidebar section that Power Users see, holding **Access & roles** and **Connectors**. The console's roles are **Power User** and **Agency**, and the Agency role doesn't see the Admin section.

**Can I create a new role?**
No. The roles are fixed, as a chip in the **Roles** panel on **Access and roles** notes. The page has no control to create, rename or edit a role.

**Can I change someone's role or business unit?**
Not in the **People** table. **Role** and **Business unit** are shown read-only. The **People** section offers **Invite person** in its header and a **Disable** button on each row, and no control to change a role or business unit.

**What happens when I disable a user, and can I re-enable them?**
To remove someone's access, click **Disable** on their row in **People**. The table offers a **Disable** button on each row and no other access controls.

**Why do agency people show Agency as their business unit?**
Agency users don't belong to a News Corp business unit, so the **Business unit** column shows **Agency** for them. Their **Role** chip also reads **Agency**.

**Can I add or delete a connector?**
The **Connectors** page has no control to add or delete a connector. From the list you can open a connector to edit it, or click **Request upload** on a **Stale** feed.

**Can I set a connector's health by hand?**
No. Health is checked automatically, and "Nobody sets health by hand". A connector's health changes only on an automatic check.

**What can I edit on a connector?**
Only the endpoint and the refresh period. Click the connector's name on **Connectors**, change **Endpoint** or **Refresh frequency** (**Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**), and click **Save**.

**Who receives the Request upload email?**
The business unit's named contact. On a **Stale** feed, "Request upload emails the unit’s named contact."
