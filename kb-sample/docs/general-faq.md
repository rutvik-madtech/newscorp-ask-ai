# General FAQ

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: All pages
Article type: faq
Category: getting_started

Answers to common questions about the News Corp Data Dexterity Layer console, whatever your role. For help on the page you are on, click **Ask AI** in the header.

**What is the News Corp Data Dexterity Layer?**
It is the console News Corp business units and agencies use to find, size, approve and share audiences across the News Corp portfolio. What you see in it depends on your role.

**Why don't the matched and modelled numbers add up? Why are they never added together?**
Matched figures count consented people found by deterministic (exact) matching. Modelled figures are people who only resemble the matched group, so the console always shows and delivers them as separately labelled audiences and never adds them together.

**Why is a size not shown?**
Sizes are shown only for groups that meet a minimum population; a group below it is suppressed and shows no value at all. Power Users see such groups marked **Suppressed** with no value. On **Portfolio audiences**, agency users see sizes that are aggregate and minimum-population gated.

**Can I export or download the list of people in an audience, or their personal data?**
No. Row-level data and raw personal data never leave the layer. At most, membership of an approved audience goes to a destination, while the attributes behind the definition and which unit each person came from are held back.

**Is anything sent without someone approving it?**
No. Nothing leaves until a person in each business unit whose data is involved approves. An expired request lapses and nothing is delivered.

**Why does my figure say "as of" a certain time?**
Sizes and counts are snapshots taken at the time shown next to them, and times are in UTC. Power Users see this, for example, as **Live · as of …** on the Overview. Agency users see it in the **Reach as of …** note under the Portfolio audiences table.

**Why can't I see a page a colleague mentioned?**
The pages you see depend on your role. Power Users see **Overview**, **Taxonomy**, **Audiences**, **Analysis**, **Rules of Use**, **Delivery** and the **Admin** section. Agency users see only **Audiences**, which opens **Portfolio audiences**.

**Can I switch the business unit I'm working as?**
Power Users choose the business unit they work as in **Acting as** at the top of the sidebar. For agency users, **Acting as** is greyed out and set to **News Corp portfolio**.

**Where do I get help?**
Click **Ask AI** in the header, on the right next to your account button. It answers questions about the page you are on, using only what your role can see.
