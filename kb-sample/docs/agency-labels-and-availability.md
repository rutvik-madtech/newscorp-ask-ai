# Labels and availability on Portfolio audiences

Applies to tier: Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: concept
Category: portfolio_audiences

On **Portfolio audiences** (Sidebar > **Audiences**), the **Label** column tells you how an audience was built, **Available on** tells you where it can be used, and the **Category** tabs group audiences by kind.

## Label: Matched

**Matched** in the **Label** column means the audience is built from matched data, found by deterministic matching. Deterministic means an exact match on an identifier, not a modelled estimate. When you click the audience, the detail panel repeats this on the size card, **Matched · deterministic**, with **Method** set to **Deterministic**.

## Available on

The **Available on** column shows where an audience can be used. Its values read:

| Value | What the label says |
|---|---|
| **Clean room** | The audience is available on a clean room. |
| **Clean room · activation** | The audience is available on a clean room and for activation. |
| **Insights only** | The audience is available for insights only. |

**Can I use this audience for activation?** Look for **Clean room · activation** in **Available on**. It is the value that names activation. **Clean room** and **Insights only** do not mention activation.

## Category

The **Category** tabs sit above the table. **Demographics**, **Intent** and **Behaviour** group audiences by category. **All** shows every audience and is selected when the page opens.

## Why it matters

- Check **Available on** before you click **Request this audience**. On approval, the audience arrives at your chosen destination.
- The **Matched** label tells you the audience comes from exact matching. When an audience is delivered, its matched and modelled portions are labelled separately and are never added together.
