# Switch the business unit you're acting as

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /
Article type: how_to
Category: overview

**Acting as** is the business unit you are working on behalf of, such as Dow Jones or Realtor.com. You change it from the **Acting as** dropdown at the top of the sidebar, on any page of the console.

## Switch business unit

1. At the top of the sidebar, under the logo, find **Acting as**.
2. Open the dropdown. It lists the business units, such as Dow Jones and Realtor.com.
3. Choose the unit you want to work as. For example, to act as Realtor.com, choose **Realtor.com**.

## What follows the unit you choose

- **Overview**: the subtitle names the unit: "What needs your decision today, then the health of everything … contributes."
- **Rules of Use**: the page title reads **Rules of Use ·** followed by the unit, and the page lists that unit's requests only.
- **Taxonomy**: the edit button under **How each business unit maps to it** is named after the unit, for example **Edit Dow Jones mapping**.
- **Ask AI**: the **Sees:** line in the panel's scope box names the business unit and role you are acting as. Ask AI sees what your role can see for that unit.

## Good to know

- Your role does not change when you switch business unit. The chip under **Acting as** still reads **Power User** with **internal · can edit**.
- Decisions sit with the unit whose data is used. To decide on a request that uses another unit's fields, a Power User acting as that unit opens **Rules of Use**. The request page heading names the unit being asked, and the page says the decision sits with that unit, not with the requester.
- If **Rules of Use** shows another unit's requests, **Acting as** is set to that unit. Check the unit named after **Rules of Use ·** in the page title, then switch **Acting as** to your own unit to see your requests.
- If the Overview shows only one business unit, that is the unit in **Acting as**. Switch unit to see another unit's requests and contribution.
