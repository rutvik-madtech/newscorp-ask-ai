# Realtor.com data in the CDL

Applies to tier: Power User, Admin, Agency
Business unit: Realtor.com
Console page: /taxonomy

Realtor.com contributes behavioural home-shopping data: what people do on Realtor.com, rather than what they declare.

## Signals
- **Actively in market**: searched or saved listings in the last 30 days.
- **High-value home interest**: viewed listings above the local price threshold.
- **Commercial listers**: people who list commercial property.

These signals appear in Analysis composition as "Realtor.com · behavioural".

## Rules that apply
- Every audience that uses Realtor.com data needs **Realtor.com approval**. The gate shows Requested until Realtor.com approves in Rules of Use.
- Behavioural signals expire after 90 days without activity.
- When Realtor.com is set as precedence in a Cross-BU audience, Realtor.com's values win on conflict.

## Typical audiences
High-net-worth households, In-market home buyers, and Small-business owners (with commercial listers).
