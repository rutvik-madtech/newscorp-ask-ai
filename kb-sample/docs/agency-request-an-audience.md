# Request a portfolio audience

Applies to tier: Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: how_to
Category: portfolio_audiences

To request an audience, open it on **Portfolio audiences** (Sidebar > **Audiences**) and click **Request this audience** in the detail panel. A person at each contributing business unit then approves or declines your request, and nothing is delivered before that.

## Request an audience

1. In the sidebar, click **Audiences** to open **Portfolio audiences**.
2. Click the audience in the table. Its details open in the panel on the right.
3. Check the **Available on** column for that audience to see where it can be used: **Clean room**, **Clean room · activation** or **Insights only**.
4. In the detail panel, read **What happens when you request**.
5. Click **Request this audience**.

## What happens after you request

**What happens when you request** lists these steps, in this order:

- "Your request goes to each contributing business unit."
- "A person there approves or declines. Nothing is delivered before that."
- "On approval the audience arrives at your chosen destination. Matched and modelled portions are labelled separately."

## Good to know

- **Who approves your request:** a person at each contributing business unit. Each one approves or declines it.
- **When you receive the audience:** only after approval. Nothing is delivered until a person at each contributing business unit has approved.
- **Matched and modelled stay apart:** modelled people, such as lookalikes, are not mixed into the matched portion. Matched and modelled portions are labelled separately on delivery and are never added together.
- **No sending to a business unit's own systems:** the box under the table lists "sending to a business unit’s own systems" as not available to your role.
- **Contributing properties stay unnamed:** Portfolio audiences does not name the News Corp properties behind an audience. **Sources** in the detail panel shows only how many contribute.
