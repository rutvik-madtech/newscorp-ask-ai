# Glossary for agency users

Applies to tier: Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: reference
Category: portfolio_audiences

This glossary explains the terms agency users see on **Portfolio audiences** (Sidebar > **Audiences**), in the sidebar and in the header. Terms are in alphabetical order.

- **Acting as**: the selector at the top of the sidebar. For agency users it is greyed out and set to **News Corp portfolio**, so you can't switch it.
- **Agency**: your role. The chip under **Acting as** reads **Agency** with **external · read and size**: you can browse, size and request audiences.
- **Aggregate**: a total count, never individual records. All sizes on Portfolio audiences are aggregate.
- **Ask AI**: the read-only help panel you open from **Ask AI** in the header. It sees only what your role can see.
- **Available on**: the table column that shows where an audience can be used: **Clean room**, **Clean room · activation** or **Insights only**.
- **Business unit**: a News Corp unit that contributes data, such as Dow Jones or Realtor.com.
- **Category**: the grouping of audiences in the tabs **Demographics**, **Intent** and **Behaviour**. The **All** tab shows every audience.
- **Contributing business unit**: a business unit whose data builds an audience. Your request goes to each one, and a person there approves or declines it.
- **Contributing properties**: the News Corp properties whose data builds an audience. They are never named. **Sources** in the detail panel shows only how many there are.
- **Destination**: where an approved audience arrives. The detail panel reads "On approval the audience arrives at your chosen destination."
- **Deterministic**: an exact match on an identifier, not modelled or estimated. You see it as **Method**: **Deterministic** and on the **Matched · deterministic** size card.
- **Matched**: people found by deterministic matching. It appears in the **Label** column.
- **Minimum-population gated**: a size is shown only when the audience meets a minimum population. The detail panel note reads "Sizes are aggregate and minimum-population gated."
- **Modelled**: people estimated by a model, sometimes called lookalikes. On delivery, modelled portions are labelled separately from matched and never added together.
- **News Corp portfolio**: the News Corp business units taken together. It is the fixed value of **Acting as** for agency users.
- **Portfolio audiences**: the page where you find, size and request audiences. Open it from Sidebar > **Audiences**.
- **Reach**: the audience size shown in the table. It is the same size as the **Matched · deterministic** card, and the **Reach as of …** note under the table shows when it was last refreshed.
