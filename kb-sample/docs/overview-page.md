# The Overview page

Applies to tier: Power User, Admin, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /

Overview is the first page after you sign in. It summarises the selected business unit's footprint in the CDL.

## What the figures mean
- **Addressable people**: people in the business unit's data who can be matched on the spine.
- **Active audiences**: audiences with the status Active.
- **Deliveries this month**: deliveries started this calendar month, whatever their status.
- **Awaiting your approval**: requests in Rules of Use that need someone in your business unit to act. Shown to Power Users and Admins only.

## Why portfolio figures change
Portfolio figures are deduplicated on the licensed spine, so a person who appears in several sources counts once. When the spine is refreshed or its contract changes, deduplication changes too, and the figures can move even though no data was added or removed. A note on the page shows the spine version in use.

## Figures for Agency users
Agency users see figures for audiences shared with their agency only. Any figure below the minimum population shows as "Below minimum".
