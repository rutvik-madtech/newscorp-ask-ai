# What Ask AI can see and do

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: Header > Ask AI (all pages)
Article type: concept
Category: ask_ai

Ask AI is a read-only help assistant. It answers questions about the page you are on, using only what your role can see, and its answers never change data. You open it from Header > **Ask AI** on every page.

## What Ask AI is

Ask AI explains things. It answers your questions in the **Ask AI · about this page** panel and shows where each answer came from. It does not act for you: it is read-only, and the line under the question box reads "Answers never change data."

When a word you use has no shared term, Ask AI names that word and drops it rather than guessing what you meant.

## Where you see what it covers

**Power Users** see a scope box at the top of the panel, written for the page you are on:

- **Sees:** the item on the page you are on, and what your role can see for the business unit selected in **Acting as**.
- **Cannot see:** row-level data, other units' unapproved fields and overlap percentages (shown as **overlap %**).
- **Cannot do:** the actions Ask AI won't take for you, such as approve, decline, deliver, edit or run anything.

The scope follows your role and the business unit in **Acting as**.

**Agency users:** Ask AI sees only what your role can see.

## Why it matters

- **Other business units' data:** Ask AI never sees more than your role can. For Power Users, other units' unapproved fields are on the **Cannot see:** list, so Ask AI can't answer from them.
- **Approving a request for you:** Ask AI can't do it, because it is read-only. For Power Users, approve and decline are on the **Cannot do:** list, so you make those decisions yourself.
- **Overlap percentages:** for Power Users, **overlap %** is on the **Cannot see:** list, so Ask AI won't give you an overlap percentage.
- **Row-level data:** for Power Users, row-level data is on the **Cannot see:** list, so Ask AI can't show you individual records.
- **Nothing changes:** whatever you ask, answers never change data.
