# Invite a person or disable access

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /admin/access-roles
Article type: how_to
Category: admin

You add a user or remove someone's access in the **People** section of Sidebar > Admin > **Access & roles**. Click **Invite person** to invite someone new, or click **Disable** on a person's row to disable their access.

## Invite a person

1. In the sidebar, under **Admin**, click **Access & roles**.
2. Scroll to the **People** section.
3. In the section header, click **Invite person**.

## Disable someone's access

1. In the sidebar, under **Admin**, click **Access & roles**.
2. In the **People** table, find the person's row. Check **Person**, **Business unit** and **Role** to make sure it's the right person.
3. Click **Disable** at the right of that row.

Every row has a **Disable** button, for Power Users and agency people alike.

## Change someone's role or business unit

The **People** table shows **Role** and **Business unit** read-only and offers no control to change them. Its controls for people are **Invite person** and **Disable**. The roles themselves are fixed: **Power User** and **Agency**.

## Good to know

- **People can ask for access themselves.** On the **Sign in** page, **Request access** lets someone without an account send their **Full name**, **Work email** and **Comments for the approver**. The page says: "Access is approved manually by News Corp. You’ll get an email once it’s decided."
- **Agency users.** The **People** table lists agency people alongside Power Users, with **Agency** in both **Business unit** and **Role**, and each has a **Disable** button. An agency user without access can also use **Request access** and name their agency in **Comments for the approver**.
- **Who sees this page.** **Access & roles** is in the Admin sidebar section, which Power Users see. The Agency role doesn't see the Admin section.
