# Check an audience's size and details

Applies to tier: Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: how_to
Category: portfolio_audiences

To see how big an audience is, open **Portfolio audiences** (Sidebar > **Audiences**) and click the audience in the table. Its size and details open in the panel on the right.

## Check the size and details

1. In the sidebar, click **Audiences** to open **Portfolio audiences**.
2. Click the audience's row in the table. The row is highlighted, and the detail panel on the right opens with the audience name as its heading.
3. Read the size card, **Matched · deterministic**. It shows the same size as the **Reach** column in the table.
4. Read the details under the card:
   - **Method**: **Deterministic**. The size comes from exact matching on an identifier, not from a model.
   - **Sources**: how many News Corp properties contribute to the audience. The properties themselves are not named.
   - **Refreshed**: when the size was last refreshed. The **Reach as of …** note under the table shows the same.
5. Read the note under the details: "Contributing properties are not named. Sizes are aggregate and minimum-population gated."

## Good to know

- **Sizes are aggregate.** A size is a total only, never a list of individual records.
- **Sizes are minimum-population gated.** A size is shown only when the audience meets a minimum population. If an audience does not meet it, no size is shown.
- **Sizes are not estimates.** The size card reads **Matched · deterministic**: the size comes from deterministic, exact matching, not from a model.
- **Matched and modelled are never added together.** When an audience is delivered, its matched and modelled portions are labelled separately.
- **You can't see which properties an audience comes from.** Contributing properties are not named to agency users. **Sources** tells you only how many contribute.
- **Next step.** Below the details, the panel shows **What happens when you request** and the **Request this audience** button. Read the steps before you request.
