# Sign-in and access FAQ

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /login
Article type: faq
Category: account

You sign in to the News Corp Data Dexterity Layer console on the **Sign in** page, reset a forgotten password from **Sign in** > **Forgot password?**, and ask for an account from **Sign in** > **Request access**. These answers cover the questions people ask most about logging in and getting access.

**Which email do I sign in with?**
The work email your access was approved for. The **Sign in** page says: "Use the work email your access was approved for."

**Is there single sign-on or another way to log in?**
The **Sign in** page asks only for **Email** and **Password**, then you click **Sign in**. It shows no other sign-in option.

**Do I need approval before I can sign in?**
Yes. You need approved access before you can sign in. If you don't have it, click **Request access** on the **Sign in** page.

**Who approves access requests?**
News Corp approves every access request manually. The **Request access** page says: "Access is approved manually by News Corp. You’ll get an email once it’s decided."

**How will I know my access request was decided?**
By email, sent to the work email you entered. The console has no status page for a pending request.

**What should I write in Comments for the approver?**
Your business unit or agency, your role, and what you need access for, as the field's hint asks.

**How do I ask for the right role?**
The console's roles are **Power User**, for internal News Corp users who work as a business unit, and **Agency**, for external agency users. Say which applies to you in **Comments for the approver** when you request access.

**Where do I reset my password?**
On **Sign in**, click **Forgot password?**, type your work email on **Reset your password** and click **Send reset link**. The link expires after a set time, so request a new one if it stops working.

**How do I sign out (log out) or find my account?**
Use the round account button with your initials at the top right of the header. It is on every page of the console, for Power Users and agency users alike.
