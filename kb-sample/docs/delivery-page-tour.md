# Find the Delivery page

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /delivery
Article type: how_to
Category: delivery

The Delivery page shows where an audience definition can be sent and what has already been sent. Open it from Sidebar > **Delivery**, or from the audience itself: Sidebar > **Audiences** > click the definition > **Deliver**. Only Power Users can open Delivery.

## Open the Delivery page

1. In the sidebar, click **Delivery**. It sits below **Rules of Use** and above the **Admin** section.
2. To open delivery for a specific audience, click **Audiences** in the sidebar, click the definition in the list, then click **Deliver** in its detail panel, next to **Duplicate**.
3. Check the heading. It reads **Delivery ·** followed by the definition's name, with the subtitle "Where this definition can go, and what has already been sent."

## What's on the Delivery page

- **Buttons at the top**: **Preview** and the **Deliver to …** button, which names the destination it sends to.
- **Destination class cards**: **Class 1** to **Class 4**, for **Your own lake / S3**, **Activation**, **Curation** and **External clean room**. Each card says how that class works and shows a readiness chip: **Ready**, **Batch drop** or **Vendor TBC**. The caption **Destination readiness checked …** under the cards shows when readiness was last checked, in UTC.
- **A red alert banner** when a delivery failed. It names the definition and the destination it didn't reach, and offers **Retry** and **Notify connector owner**.
- **In flight and recent**, marked **live** with a time. It lists deliveries with their **Definition**, **Destination**, **Label**, **Status** and **Started (UTC)**, across definitions, not only the one in the heading.
- **Permissible-use envelope**, on the right. It lists the usage rules that go out with every delivery: "Travels with every delivery and comes back with any measurement."
- **Agent endpoint**, below the envelope. It summarises the endpoint AI agents use to ask about audiences, with **Agents**, **Requests** and **Awaiting you**: "Returns size, composition and delivery options. It never transacts, and nothing is sent until a person approves."

## Good to know

- Can agency users see deliveries? No. Delivery is a Power User page. Agency users don't see it; their sidebar shows only **Audiences**, which opens Portfolio audiences.
- The page covers the definition named in its heading. To deliver a different audience, open that definition from **Audiences** and click **Deliver**.
- Raw personal data never leaves. The envelope shows **Raw PII outbound** as **Never**.
- Nothing leaves until a person in each business unit whose data is involved approves.
