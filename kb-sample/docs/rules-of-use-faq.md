# Rules of Use FAQ

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /rules-of-use
Article type: faq
Category: rules_of_use

Answers to common questions about Sidebar > **Rules of Use**, where your business unit approves or declines requests to use its fields.

**Can the requester approve their own request?**
No. The decision sits with the business unit whose fields are used, not with the requester. A Power User acting as that unit in **Acting as** approves or declines the request on its **Rules of Use** page.

**Does my approval cover other variants or destinations?**
No. “Approving this covers this audience, this destination and this variant,” and the **Approve request** dialog shows the audience **pinned to** a version. Sending an approved audience to another destination class arrives as a separate **New destination** request.

**What is recorded when I approve or decline?**
“Every decision is recorded with the approver’s name and the time.” Declining a delivery is also recorded with your reason. **Recent decisions** lists each decision as **Approved** or **Declined**, with the audience, its destination and the date.

**What does “Your … values lose on conflict” mean?**
On a **Cross-BU output** card, an amber notice starts **Precedence set by the creator:** and names the attribute and the business unit that wins. The creator of the output set that unit's values to win on that attribute, so where your values differ, theirs are used.

**Why does an agency agent want our fields, and what does MCP mean?**
**Agent request · MCP** means an agency's buyer agent sent the request through MCP, to size or receive an audience that uses your fields. The **Approve request** dialog marks the requester **(credentialed, MCP)**, so the agent is credentialed. The **Brief:** line shows what the agent asked for, and nothing is sent until you approve.

**Is Data steward a role?**
No. **Data steward** is a job function shown next to the requester's business unit on **Cross-BU output** and **New destination** cards. The console's roles are Power User and Agency.

**What does Retention there mean?**
In the **Approve request** dialog, **Retention there** is how long the destination may keep the data.

**Can I edit, add or delete our gating rules?**
No. **Your gating rules** shows a **Fixed this phase** chip, so the rules can't be changed on this page. Click **Request a rule change** to ask for a change.

**Can I undo a decline?**
No. “Declining is final for this request. Nothing is delivered, and the decision is recorded with your name and the time.” Until you click **Decline request**, **Cancel** or × closes the dialog and the request stays pending.
