# Read your unit's contribution on the Overview

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /
Article type: how_to
Category: overview

The **Overview** page (Sidebar > **Overview**) shows the health of everything your business unit contributes. Next to the **Awaiting your approval** card at the top, the **Active definitions** and **Portfolio reachable · matched** cards show how many definitions are live and how many matched people the portfolio can reach; below them, the **Largest definitions** table lists your unit's largest definitions. Everything on the page is for the unit in **Acting as**.

## Read the figures

1. In the sidebar, click **Overview**. The subtitle names the unit you are acting as: "What needs your decision today, then the health of everything … contributes."
2. Read the **Active definitions** card. It shows how many definitions are live. The line under it, **Live · as of …**, gives the time the figure is current to. Times are in UTC.
3. Read the **Portfolio reachable · matched** card. It shows how many matched people can be reached across the portfolio. If the line under it reads **Pending spine**, the deduplicated portfolio figure is not available, so a placeholder shows instead of a value.
4. Read the **Largest definitions** table. Its columns are:
   - **Definition**: the audience definition's name.
   - **Type**: **Single BU** (built on one business unit's data), **Cross-BU** (combines more than one business unit) or **Cross-BU reach**.
   - **Owner**: the business unit that owns the definition.
   - **Portfolio · matched**: consented, matched people, deduplicated on the spine.
   - **Modelled · separate**: the definition's modelled extension, shown with a plus sign. A dash means the definition has no modelled extension.
5. Click a definition name to open it in **Audiences**, or click the **All …** link at the top right of the table to open the full list.

## Good to know

- The note under the table reads "Consented, matched people, deduplicated on the spine, as of … Modelled extensions sit in their own column and are never added in." Matched and modelled figures are never added together, so read each column on its own.
- The spine is the identity spine, which matches people and removes duplicates across business units. The portfolio figure depends on it, which is why the card can read **Pending spine** and show a placeholder instead of a value.
- Each figure is current as of the time shown with it.
- To see another unit's contribution, switch **Acting as** at the top of the sidebar.
- For what each **Type** means in detail, including **Cross-BU reach**, see Audience types, precedence and statuses.
