# Ask AI FAQ

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: Header > Ask AI (all pages)
Article type: faq
Category: ask_ai

Answers to common questions about **Ask AI**, the help assistant in the console header. Click Header > **Ask AI**, to the right of the search box, to open the **Ask AI · about this page** panel on any page.

**Does Ask AI work on every page?**
Yes. **Ask AI** is in the header on every page, and it answers about the page you are on. The panel heading reads **Ask AI · about this page**.

**Can agency users use Ask AI?**
Yes. Agency users have the same **Ask AI** button in the header. Ask AI sees only what your role can see.

**Can Ask AI change my audience or approve a request for me?**
No. Ask AI is read-only, and the line under the question box reads "Answers never change data." Power Users see this spelled out on the **Cannot do:** line at the top of the panel.

**Why did Ask AI ignore or drop one of my words?**
That word has no shared term. Ask AI names the word and drops it rather than guessing what you meant.

**Can Ask AI show row-level data?**
No. Row-level data is outside what Ask AI can see.

**Are Ask AI's figures estimates?**
Answers marked **not an estimate** come from records, not modelled figures. They are as of the time shown beside **AI answer**, in UTC.

**The Ask AI answer is wrong. How do I report it?**
Check the **Based on:** sources and the as-of time next to **AI answer**. Then click **Not right** under the answer.

**Ask AI says it can't see something. Why?**
That information is outside what your role can see. Power Users can check the **Cannot see:** list at the top of the panel, which names row-level data, other units' unapproved fields and **overlap %**.

**Can I make the Ask AI panel bigger, or close it?**
Yes. Click **Expand panel** (⤢) at the top right of the panel to make it bigger. Click **×** (**Close Ask AI**) to close it and go back to the page.
