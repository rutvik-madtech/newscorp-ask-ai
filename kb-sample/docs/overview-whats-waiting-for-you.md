# See what's waiting for your decision

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /
Article type: how_to
Category: overview

The **Overview** page (Sidebar > **Overview**) opens with the **Awaiting your approval** card at the top, which shows how many requests are waiting for your decision. Further down, on the right beside **Largest definitions**, the **Waiting on you** panel lists them. Both are scoped to the business unit in **Acting as**. You see the requests here, and you approve or decline them on **Rules of Use**.

## Check what's waiting

1. In the sidebar, click **Overview**. Check that **Acting as** shows the unit you want; the page subtitle names it.
2. Read the **Awaiting your approval** card at the top. It shows how many requests are waiting for you, and **Oldest expires in …** shows how long the oldest one has left.
3. To act on them, click **Open Rules of Use** on the card.
4. For the detail, read the **Waiting on you** panel on the right, beside **Largest definitions**. Each item shows:
   - the request kind and the audience: **Agent request · …**, **Cross-BU output · …** or **New destination · …**
   - who is asking and where the audience would go, written as requester → destination
   - where it applies, how many of your unit's fields the request uses (**… of your fields**)
   - a chip on the right: a red **Expires in …** chip marks a request close to expiring, and a grey **Received …** chip shows when the request arrived.
5. Click **Open all** to see every request on **Rules of Use**, then approve or decline each one there.

## Good to know

- You can't approve or decline from the Overview. Use **Open Rules of Use** or **Open all**, and make your decision on the Rules of Use page.
- The request kinds: **Agent request** is an agency's buyer agent asking to use your fields; **Cross-BU output** is another business unit wanting an output that uses your fields; **New destination** is an audience to be sent to a new destination.
- Deal with red **Expires in …** items first. Nothing is delivered on a timeout: an expired request lapses rather than passing.
- When nothing needs you, the panel shows **Nothing waiting on you** with the text "When another unit or an agent asks to use your fields, the request appears here. Nothing is sent until you approve." Its **Review your gating rules** link opens Rules of Use, where **Your gating rules** are listed.
- If you expected a request and see none, check **Acting as**. The Overview only shows requests for the unit you are acting as.
- The amber number on **Rules of Use** in the sidebar also counts the requests awaiting you.
