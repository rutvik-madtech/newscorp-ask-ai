# Check, copy and rate an Ask AI answer

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: Header > Ask AI (all pages)
Article type: how_to
Category: ask_ai

Every Ask AI answer tells you what it came from and how current it is, so you can check it before you use it. Open Header > **Ask AI**, ask a question, then use the lines and buttons around the **AI answer** chip to check the sources, copy the answer or rate it.

## How an answer is laid out

- The **AI answer** chip, with a line next to it saying what the answer came from and when, for example **From records as of … · not an estimate** or **From the shared vocabulary as of …**.
- A short summary in bold.
- Bullet points with the detail.
- A **Based on:** line listing the sources.
- Action buttons under the answer.

## Check, copy and rate an answer

1. Find the **AI answer** chip above the answer and read the line next to it. It tells you where the answer came from and the time it is as of. Times are in UTC.
2. Read the **Based on:** line under the answer. It lists the sources the answer used.
3. To check a source, click it. Some sources are links that open the page the source comes from; others are plain text.
4. To reuse the answer, click **Copy**.
5. To rate the answer, click **Helpful** if it answered your question, or **Not right** if it is wrong.

## Good to know

- **not an estimate** means the answer comes from records, not from modelled figures.
- The as-of time next to **AI answer** shows how current the records behind the answer are.
- If an answer looks wrong, compare it with its **Based on:** sources and its as-of time, then click **Not right** to report it.
- Power Users also see **Save to definition notes** under answers about an audience definition.
- Ask AI is read-only. The line under the question box reads "Answers never change data."
