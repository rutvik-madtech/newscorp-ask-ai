# Ask a portfolio question

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /analysis
Article type: how_to
Category: analysis

To ask a question across the whole News Corp portfolio, go to Sidebar > **Analysis** > **Portfolio** and use the **Ask a portfolio question** card at the top of the tab. You type the question in plain language, check how your words resolve to shared terms, and only then run it.

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, click **Analysis**, then click the **Portfolio** tab. The page subtitle reads "Portfolio-wide answers with no advertiser file involved."
2. In the **Ask a portfolio question** card, type your question in **Your question, in plain language**. The card shows a **Draft** chip until the question runs.
3. Optional: check your wording with Ask AI first (see below).
4. Click **Resolve terms**. "Nothing runs until you have seen the terms." The **Terms, before anything runs** screen opens and shows how each of your words resolved.
5. Check the terms, then click **Run the question** on that screen. To change the wording instead, click **Edit the question**, which brings you back to this card.

## Check your wording with Ask AI

1. Click **Ask AI** in the header. The **Ask AI · about this page** panel opens on the right.
2. Under **Try asking**, click **Which of my words map to a shared term?**. The answer, marked **AI answer**, lists which of your phrases map to a shared term and names any word that has none, which is dropped rather than guessed. **Based on:** links to its source, **Taxonomy · canonical terms**.
3. **Try asking** also offers **What will this question not be able to answer?**. You can also type your own question in **Ask about this page** and click **Send**.

The panel's scope box sets its limits:

- **Sees:** the shared vocabulary, and counts you are already allowed to see as a Power User of the unit you are acting as.
- **Cannot see:** row-level data, other units’ unapproved fields, overlap %.
- **Cannot do:** run, approve, deliver or edit anything.

"Answers never change data."

## Good to know

The **This kind of question** box on the card sets out the rules:

- "Takes no advertiser file and no identifiers from you."
- "Returns counts only. It creates no audience and sends nothing anywhere." To build an audience from the answer, use **Turn it into an audience** on the screen that opens when you click **What happens next** on **The finding**, after the question runs. Each contributing unit is asked first.
- "Counts only people eligible for cross-unit use."
- To match an uploaded customer list instead, use the **Runs** tab.
