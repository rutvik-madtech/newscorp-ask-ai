# Save, turn into an audience or export a portfolio question

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /analysis
Article type: how_to
Category: analysis

To save a portfolio question, turn it into an audience or export its counts, click **What happens next** on **The finding** (Sidebar > **Analysis** > **Portfolio** > your question > **See the finding**). The screen opens with the heading "You have an answer. Nothing left the building." and the breadcrumb **Portfolio › Question … › Outcome**.

## What the question produced

The page confirms that the question ran across business units and produced no destination, no file and no identifiers. The summary cards read:

- **Destinations used**: No adapter was called.
- **Files produced**: Counts on screen only.
- **Identifiers you supplied**: No advertiser file involved.
- **Audiences created**: A question is not an audience.

## Steps

The table **What you can do from here** lists each **Action**, **What it does** and its **Gate**, the approval the action needs.

**Save the question**

1. Find the **Save the question** row. It "Keeps the wording and the term mapping. Keeps no people." Gate: **None**.
2. Click **Save**.

**Turn it into an audience**

1. Find the **Turn it into an audience** row. It "Starts a separate, buyable definition. Each contributing unit is asked first." Gate: **Unit approval**.
2. Click **Start**. **Create an audience** opens.

**Export the counts**

1. Find the **Export the counts** row. It "Sends aggregate figures outside the console." Gate: **Unit approval**.
2. Click **Request approval**. This button can show greyed out, and the page shows no reason next to it.

## Good to know

- "Reading a count inside the console needs no approval. Anything that leaves the console does."
- **None** means no approval is needed. **Unit approval** means each contributing business unit must approve first. Nothing leaves until a person in each business unit whose data is involved approves, in Sidebar > **Rules of Use**.
- A question is not an audience. Saving it keeps the wording and term mapping but no people.
- Click **Show what the layer kept** to see the custody record for the query. Next to the button, the page notes that the query has closed and when.
