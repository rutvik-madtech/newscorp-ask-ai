# Check how your words resolved

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /analysis
Article type: how_to
Category: analysis

After you click **Resolve terms** on Sidebar > **Analysis** > **Portfolio**, the **Terms, before anything runs** screen shows how each word in your question maps to a shared term and to each business unit's own field. The chip **Nothing has been evaluated yet** confirms that nothing has run, so you can still run the question or edit it.

The breadcrumb reads **Portfolio › Question … · draft**, and your question is shown at the top in quotation marks. Click **Portfolio** in the breadcrumb to go back to the Portfolio tab.

## Steps

1. Read **How your words resolved**. Each row shows:
   - **Your words**: the phrase from your question.
   - **Shared term**: the shared term the phrase resolves to.
   - A local field column for each unit, such as **Dow Jones local field** and **Realtor.com local field**: the field that unit will evaluate.
   - **Status**: how the phrase resolved.
2. Read **Who will be counted** (see below).
3. In the **Run it** card, click **Run the question**. The result screen, **Evaluated at each unit, then resolved**, opens.
4. To change your wording, click **Edit the question**. You go back to the **Ask a portfolio question** card, where you can edit the text and click **Resolve terms** again.

## What the statuses mean

- **Resolved** (green): the phrase maps to a shared term.
- **Dropped, not approximated** (highlighted row): the word has no shared term. Its **Shared term** cell reads **No shared term** and its local field cells show a dash (—). "A word with no shared term is removed from the question and named here. It is never mapped to the nearest available field."
- **No equivalent**, in a unit's local field column: that unit has no local field for the shared term.

Each unit keeps its own field naming. The shared term is mapped onto each unit's local field.

## Who is counted

The **Who will be counted** card states:

- "People eligible for cross-unit use only."
- "Opted-out records are excluded at each unit before counting."
- The geographic scope the count covers.
- "The answer is a count of the eligible population, and it is labelled that way on the result."

## Good to know

- Each unit evaluates the question in its own environment first, and nothing is matched until every unit has answered.
- If a word you need was dropped, click **Edit the question** and reword it. The shared terms are the canonical terms in Sidebar > **Taxonomy**.
- Before you resolve, Ask AI can answer **Which of my words map to a shared term?** on the Portfolio tab.
