# Getting started for agency users

Applies to tier: Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /audiences
Article type: getting_started
Category: getting_started

As an agency user, you use the News Corp Data Dexterity Layer console to find an audience across the News Corp portfolio, see its size, and request it. Everything you need is under Sidebar > **Audiences**, which opens **Portfolio audiences**.

## Sign in

On the **Sign in** page, enter the work email your access was approved for and your password, then click **Sign in**. If you have no access, click **Request access** on the same page. Access is approved manually by News Corp.

## Your role

The chip under **Acting as** in the sidebar reads **Agency** with **external · read and size**. You can browse portfolio audiences, see their sizes and request them. You can't create or edit anything.

## What you see

- **Acting as** is greyed out and set to **News Corp portfolio**. You can't switch it.
- **Audiences** is the only item in your sidebar. Under it, a note reads "Overview, Taxonomy, Analysis, Rules of Use, Delivery and Admin are business-unit surfaces and are not rendered for your role." That is why you see only **Audiences**.
- **Portfolio audiences** opens with "Find an audience, size it, and request it. Contributing properties are not named." It has **Category** tabs, a table with **Audience**, **Label**, **Reach** and **Available on** columns, and a detail panel on the right for the audience you select.
- A box under the table lists what is "Not available to your role: creating or editing definitions, setting precedence, changing the taxonomy, or sending to a business unit’s own systems."
- The header has the search box, **Ask AI** and your account button. Ask AI sees only what your role can see.

## Get your first audience

1. In the sidebar, click **Audiences**.
2. Pick a **Category** tab: **All**, **Demographics**, **Intent** or **Behaviour**.
3. Click an audience in the table. Its details open in the panel on the right.
4. Check its size on the **Matched · deterministic** card, along with **Method**, **Sources** and **Refreshed**.
5. Check the **Available on** column to see where the audience can be used.
6. Read **What happens when you request**, then click **Request this audience**.

## Good to know

- Your request goes to each contributing business unit. A person there approves or declines it, and nothing is delivered before that.
- On approval, the audience arrives at your chosen destination. Matched and modelled portions are labelled separately.
- Contributing properties are not named. **Sources** in the detail panel describes them without naming them.
- Sizes are aggregate and minimum-population gated. The **Reach as of …** note under the table gives the time the figures were taken, in UTC.
