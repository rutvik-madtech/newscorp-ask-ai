# Reset your password

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /reset-password
Article type: how_to
Category: account

If you forgot your password, ask for an emailed reset link on the **Reset your password** page. To get there, go to **Sign in** > **Forgot password?**. The page reads: "Enter your email and we’ll send a link to set a new password."

## Reset your password

1. On the **Sign in** page, click **Forgot password?**. It sits under the **Sign in** button, on the left.
2. On **Reset your password**, type your work email in **Email**.
3. Click **Send reset link**.
4. Open the email you receive and use the link to set a new password.
5. Go back to the **Sign in** page and sign in (log in) with your new password.

## Good to know

- **The reset link expires.** The link works only for a set time. If it no longer works, go back to **Sign in** > **Forgot password?** and request a new one with **Send reset link**.
- **The page never says whether your email is registered.** Under **Send reset link** the page shows: "If the address has an account, a link is on its way. For privacy, we show this message either way." Seeing this message does not confirm that the address has an account.
- **Use your approved work email.** Type the work email your access was approved for, because that is the address you sign in with.
- **Leave without resetting.** To return to the **Sign in** page without sending a link, click **Back to sign in**.
- **No access at all?** A password reset does not give you access. If you were never approved, use **Request access** on the **Sign in** page instead.
