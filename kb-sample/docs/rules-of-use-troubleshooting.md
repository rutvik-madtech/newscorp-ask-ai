# I can't find a request in Rules of Use

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /rules-of-use
Article type: troubleshooting
Category: rules_of_use

Sidebar > **Rules of Use** lists the requests waiting for the business unit in **Acting as**. If a request isn't there, check the unit you are acting as, the **Request type** tab, and whether the request was decided or ran out of time.

## The request isn't on your list

**What you see:** the page title reads **Rules of Use ·** followed by a unit, and the request isn't among the cards. For example, you are acting as Dow Jones and can't find a request that uses Realtor.com fields.

**Why it happens:** the page lists requests for the unit in **Acting as** only. A request goes to the business unit whose fields it uses, not to the requester.

**What to do:** at the top of the sidebar, switch **Acting as** to the unit whose fields the request uses, such as Realtor.com. If you sent the request yourself, follow it on **From a seed**, where the approval gate named after the other unit, such as **Realtor.com approval**, shows **Pending**.

## Only some requests show

**What you see:** fewer request cards than you expect.

**Why it happens:** a **Request type** tab other than **All** is selected: **Agent**, **Cross-BU** or **Destination**.

**What to do:** click **All**. Each tab shows how many requests it holds.

## The list says Nothing waiting on you

**What you see:** **Nothing waiting on you**, with “When another unit or an agent asks to use your fields, the request appears here.”

**Why it happens:** no requests need a decision from the unit in **Acting as**.

**What to do:** check that **Acting as** shows the right unit. **Review your gating rules** opens Rules of Use, where **Your gating rules** are listed.

## The request has gone

**What you see:** a request you saw earlier, perhaps with a red **Expires in …** chip, is no longer waiting for you.

**Why it happens:** someone decided it, or its time ran out. “Nothing is delivered on a timeout, and an expired request lapses rather than passing.”

**What to do:** **Recent decisions** on Rules of Use lists requests marked **Approved** or **Declined**, with the audience, its destination and the date. A request that lapsed was not approved, and nothing was delivered.

## You saw it on the Overview

**What you see:** the request is in **Waiting on you** on the **Overview**.

**Why it happens:** you approve or decline on Rules of Use, not on the Overview.

**What to do:** click **Open all** in **Waiting on you**, or **Open Rules of Use** on the **Awaiting your approval** card.
