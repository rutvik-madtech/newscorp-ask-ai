# What each role can see

Applies to tier: Power User, Admin, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /

The CDL console has three roles: Power User, Admin and Agency. An Admin sets your role, and it is shown in the user menu.

| Page or feature | Power User | Admin | Agency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overview, Taxonomy | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Audiences: build and view | Yes | Yes | Audiences shared with your agency |
| Analysis | Yes | Yes | Your own runs |
| Delivery: track deliveries | Yes | Yes | Deliveries of audiences shared with you |
| Rules of Use: approve and decline requests | Yes | Yes | No |
| Consent ledger, Audit trail | Read only | Yes | No |
| Access & roles, Connectors | No | Yes | No |

## What Agency users don't see
Agency partners work with audiences that News Corp business units share with them. To protect each business unit's data, the console withholds some details from the Agency role:
- the names of contributing business units when source obfuscation is on, shown as "Contributing business unit";
- cross-business-unit overlap percentages, such as the share of people matched in both Dow Jones and Realtor.com data;
- audience sizes below the minimum population, shown as "Below minimum";
- record-level data of any kind.

Raw personal data (names, emails, addresses) is never shown to any role and never leaves the CDL.

## Getting more access
If you need a page your role doesn't include, ask an Admin in your business unit. Admins change roles in Access & roles.
