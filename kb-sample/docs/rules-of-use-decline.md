# Decline a request

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /rules-of-use
Article type: how_to
Category: rules_of_use

To decline (reject) a request, click **Decline** on the request card in Sidebar > **Rules of Use**, or under **Your decision** on a delivery request page. The **Decline request** dialog lets you pick a reason and send the requester an explanation.

## Decline a request

1. In the sidebar, set **Acting as** to your business unit and click **Rules of Use**.
2. On the request card, or on a delivery request page, click **Decline**. The **Decline request** dialog opens. The line at the top shows the requester, the audience and your fields involved.
3. Under **Reason**, select one:
   - **A field is not licensed for onward distribution**
   - **This destination is not permitted for these fields**
   - **The audience is too small for this use**
   - **Something else**
4. Read **Message sent to the requester**. It is tagged **AI draft · edit before sending**: the console drafts it from the relevant gating rule and the reason you picked. Edit the text as needed.
5. Leave **Send this message to the requester** ticked to send the message, or untick it to decline without a message.
6. Click **Decline request**.

## Good to know

- “Declining is final for this request. Nothing is delivered, and the decision is recorded with your name and the time.” You can't undo a decline.
- Nothing is sent until you click **Decline request**.
- **Cancel** or × closes the dialog. The request stays pending and nothing is sent.
- **Send this message to the requester** is ticked when the dialog opens. If you untick it, the decline is still recorded with your name and the time.
- The draft is AI-written. Check it says what you mean before sending.
- On a delivery request page: “Declining stops this delivery and is recorded with your reason.”
- To see the rules the draft draws on, look at **Your gating rules** on the Rules of Use page.
