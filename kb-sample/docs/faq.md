# Frequently asked questions

Applies to tier: Power User, Admin, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /

**Why don't matched and modelled sizes add up to one total?**
They are counted separately and never added together. See "Matched, modelled and definitional audiences".

**Why can't I send my audience for approval?**
Only Draft audiences can be sent for approval, and the Minimum population gate must pass first.

**Why can't I duplicate an audience?**
Only Active or Pending approval audiences can be duplicated.

**My delivery says "Held — approval". What does that mean?**
A contributing business unit hasn't approved the use yet. See "Troubleshooting a delivery".

**Can I download the people in an audience?**
No. The CDL never exports record-level data or raw personal data. Deliveries go to approved destinations only.

**Why did the Overview figures change overnight?**
Portfolio figures are deduplicated on the licensed spine, and they change when the spine is refreshed.

**What files can I upload to Analysis?**
CSV files with one identifier column: hashed email, ID5 ID or LUID.

**Can Ask AI tell me how big my audience is?**
No. Ask AI answers from the product documentation and can't see live figures. Open the audience on the Audiences page to see its size.
