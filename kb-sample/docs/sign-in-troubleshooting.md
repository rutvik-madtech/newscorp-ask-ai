# Can't sign in or didn't get a reset email

Applies to tier: Power User, Agency
Business unit: all
Console page: /login
Article type: troubleshooting
Category: account

If you can't sign in (log in) or a password reset email hasn't arrived, the fixes are on the sign-in pages: **Sign in**, **Reset your password** (from **Sign in** > **Forgot password?**) and **Request access** (from **Sign in** > **Request access**). Find your symptom below.

## You can't sign in

**What you see:** you are stuck on the **Sign in** page and can't get into the console.

**Why it happens:** you sign in with the work email your access was approved for and your password. You also need approved access before you can sign in at all.

**What to do:**
- Check that you typed the work email your access was approved for in **Email**, not a personal or other address.
- If you forgot your password, click **Forgot password?** and reset it.
- If you were never approved, click **Request access** and send a request.

## No reset email arrived

**What you see:** you clicked **Send reset link** but no email has arrived.

**Why it happens:** a link goes out only if the address has an account, so a mistyped address means no email reaches you. The page shows the same message whether or not the address has an account: "If the address has an account, a link is on its way. For privacy, we show this message either way." The message is not proof that your address is registered.

**What to do:** check that you typed the work email your access was approved for. Then go back to **Sign in** > **Forgot password?** and click **Send reset link** again.

## Your reset link stopped working

**What you see:** the link in your reset email no longer works.

**Why it happens:** reset links expire after a set time.

**What to do:** request a new link. On **Sign in**, click **Forgot password?**, type your work email in **Email** and click **Send reset link**.

## You requested access but heard nothing

**What you see:** you sent a request from **Request access** and have no answer.

**Why it happens:** "Access is approved manually by News Corp. You’ll get an email once it’s decided." The decision arrives by email, and the console has no status page for a pending request.

**What to do:** watch the inbox of the work email you entered in **Work email**. Until you are approved, you can't sign in.
