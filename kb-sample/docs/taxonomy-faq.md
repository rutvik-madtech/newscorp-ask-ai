# Taxonomy FAQ

Applies to tier: Power User
Business unit: all
Console page: /taxonomy
Article type: faq
Category: taxonomy

Answers to common questions about Sidebar > **Taxonomy**, the shared vocabulary of canonical terms that sits above each business unit's own taxonomy.

**Does mapping a term change our local taxonomy?**
No. Local taxonomies are listed under **Local · read only** and are never modified. The page subtitle reads "Canonical terms sit above each unit’s own taxonomy and never overwrite it."

**Why is a business unit marked No equivalent instead of a close match?**
The unit has no local term or fields that match the canonical term, and the console never approximates with a proxy. Under **How each business unit maps to it**, that unit's **Source fields** cell says why.

**Can I edit another business unit's mapping?**
The edit button is named after the unit in **Acting as**, for example **Edit Dow Jones mapping**, and it is the only edit button on the term. The other units' rows have no edit button.

**Why do the local counts say as of last sync?**
The term counts under **Local · read only** reflect the last sync. The note **Counts as of last sync** shows that time, in UTC.

**Is the term size live?**
No. **Portfolio · matched** is a snapshot as of the time in the note **Size as of … · use count live**. The **In use by** count, which shows how many audiences use the term, is live.

**Do delivered audiences change if a term changes?**
No. "Delivered audiences stay pinned to the version that produced them, so returned measurement stays traceable." Each change shows as a new version in **Version history**.

**Can agency users see or change the taxonomy?**
No. The Taxonomy page is not shown to the Agency role, and changing the taxonomy is listed as not available to that role. Agency users see category names only as filter tabs on **Portfolio audiences**.

**Why was a word in my portfolio question dropped?**
It has no shared term. On Sidebar > **Analysis** > **Portfolio** > **Resolve terms**, the word is removed from the question, named under **How your words resolved** and marked **Dropped, not approximated**, never mapped to the nearest available field. Click **Edit the question** to reword it.
