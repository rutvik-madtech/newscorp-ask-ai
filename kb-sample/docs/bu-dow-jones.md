# Dow Jones data in the CDL

Applies to tier: Power User, Admin, Agency
Business unit: Dow Jones
Console page: /taxonomy

Dow Jones contributes declared professional data: what subscribers tell Dow Jones about their work.

## Attributes
- JOB_TITLE and JOB_FUNCTION, for example "Chief Financial Officer" and "Finance".
- INDUSTRY and COMPANY_SIZE, in employee bands.
- NET_WORTH_RANGE, declared at subscription, in bands.

These attributes appear in Analysis composition as "Dow Jones · declared".

## Rules that apply
- JOB_TITLE never leaves the CDL as a field. It can inform a cohort, such as "C-suite decision-makers", but a delivery can't include it.
- "Small-business owners" uses Dow Jones company size, with a ceiling of 50 employees.
- Dow Jones approves cross-BU use of its fields in Rules of Use, usually within one business day.

## Typical audiences
C-suite decision-makers, Senior finance decision-makers, Small-business owners and Frequent business travellers.
